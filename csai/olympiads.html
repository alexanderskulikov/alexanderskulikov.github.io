Olympiads

Competition training is part of the academic culture.

Students prepare for programming, mathematics, and research competitions with peers, faculty, and engineers who understand high-level contest work.

Tracks

Three complementary competition paths.

Programming competitions

Students train for ICPC-style problem solving, algorithms, data structures, and implementation under time pressure.

Mathematical competitions

At IMC 2026, NUP received two gold and three silver medals and placed 16th worldwide.

Research competitions

The NUP team won first place in the IWLS Programming Contest in 2024 and 2026.

Achievements

Students work at international level.

The student community includes participants of IMO, IOI, IBO, OWAO, ICPC, IMC, and research competitions. NUP is represented in ICPC World Finals-level contests, IMC medal results, and the IWLS programming contest.

ICPC World Finals

NUP is the only university from Cyprus whose team advanced to the ICPC World Finals.

IMC medals

At IMC 2026, NUP received two gold and three silver medals and placed 16th worldwide.

IWLS winner

The NUP team won first place in the IWLS Programming Contest in 2024 and 2026.

Algorithms, discrete mathematics, probability, programming practice, theoretical computer science, and project courses give students the technical base for competition and research work.
