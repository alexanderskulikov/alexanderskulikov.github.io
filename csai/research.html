Research

Research starts early and connects to real open problems.

Students can explore algorithms, machine learning, bioinformatics, complexity theory, programming languages, robotics, and AI systems with researchers and engineers.

Directions

Research areas students can grow into.

Personal satisfaction

Research gives students the experience of exploring uncharted territory, solving problems without existing solutions, and contributing ideas that can move a field forward.

Networking and travel

Research conferences create a direct path to meeting strong people in similar areas, discussing new work, and seeing how the international research community operates.

Further education

Strong publications improve applications to competitive M.Sc. and Ph.D. programs. Graduates of related programs have continued at EPFL, ETH, GWU, NYU, and UCSD.

Industry opportunities

In technology companies, especially R&D departments, high-impact publications can distinguish candidates and lead to more advanced roles.

Skill development

Writing a paper forces clear thinking, precise communication, careful experiments, and the ability to explain complex ideas to a wider audience.

Research areas

Students can work in algorithms, AI, bioinformatics, complexity theory, machine learning, programming languages, robotics, and HCI.
