Teaching Math: Oral Homework Defense

Students explain and defend
every homework assignment orally.

We encourage them to try solving
each problem on their own first.

AI tools are welcome.
Students must understand their solutions and answer follow-up questions.

Teaching Math:
A Flipped Classroom

  • Interactive textbook: 30 chapters, 500+ automatically graded problems (multiple choice, math, and code), and 500+ theory problems
  • Flipped format: students study at home. All class time is devoted to discussing problems and addressing gaps in understanding.
  • I first tried this format in 2017 while teaching Algorithms 202 at UCSD.

Summary and Open Problems

Our experience

  • Homework: AI is welcome in most cases. Students defend their work orally.
  • Class time: more interaction and more feedback.
  • Research: we use AI extensively.
  • Math and programming competitions: the least affected by AI.
